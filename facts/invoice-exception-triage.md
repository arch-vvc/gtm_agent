# Fact sheet: invoice exception triage demo

Repo: https://github.com/OWNER/REPLACE_ME_invoice_exception_triage
Names: invoice exception triage, invoice triage

## What it does
- invoice exception triage takes three messy exports (invoices, purchase orders and payments) such as from Tally or Zoho, flags every three way match mismatch (wrong amounts, duplicates, missing purchase orders and GST errors), shows the rupees at risk, and lets you click a row to see the raw rows and a one sentence explanation.

## What it does not measure
- GST filing validity
- bank reconciliation
- ERP write back
- fraud

## Numbers you may quote
- three exports
